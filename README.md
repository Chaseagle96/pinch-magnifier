Bring intuitive, gesture-based magnification to your GNOME desktop.  Pinch-to-Zoom lets you effortlessly zoom in and out of your screen using natural multi-touch trackpad pinch gestures. Designed with accessibility and ease of use in mind, it makes desktop magnification feel seamless, responsive, and intuitive.
